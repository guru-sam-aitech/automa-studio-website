Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 August 2026

This privacy policy explains how Decide It! ("the App", "we", "our") handles information when you use our app on the Google Play Store.

Summary

  • The App does not require an account and does not collect, upload, or transmit any personal data to us.
  • All your data (decision lists, history, app preferences) is stored only on your device.
  • The App shows advertising provided by Google AdMob and offers a paid "Remove Ads" in-app purchase processed by Google Play Billing.
  • These services may collect data under their own policies.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, or have access to:

  • Your name, email address, or any contact information.
  • Your location.
  • Your device identifiers (we never read the advertising ID or device ID).
  • The content of your decision lists, coin flips, or spin history.

All user-created content is saved in a local database on your device and never leaves it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays advertising served by Google's AdMob service, including interstitial ads and rewarded video ads. AdMob may collect and process data to serve ads, including:

  • Device advertising identifiers (subject to your choices below).
  • Approximate or coarse location (if you grant permission at the OS level; the App itself does not request or use location).
  • Data about your interaction with ads.

AdMob's data handling is governed by Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Your Consent Choices (EU / EEA / UK users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the UK, or other regions with similar laws, the App uses the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent before personalized advertising begins. You may accept or decline; declining does not prevent the App from working, and ads (if shown) will not be personalized. You can change your consent choices at any time via the App's Settings screen.

4. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The App offers a one-time "Remove Ads" purchase. Purchases are processed and recorded by Google Play. Google's handling of your purchase data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

If you reinstall the App on the same Google account, your purchase is automatically restored.

5. Children

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. AdMob serves the App in accordance with the Families Policy self-certification configured in the Play Console.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete all of it at any time by:

  • Clearing the App's data/storage in your device Settings, or
  • Uninstalling the App.

We hold no copies of your data and cannot access it.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted to this page, and the "Last updated" date above will be revised.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact: hello@automatestudio.ie
