Decide It! — Decision Wheel & Coin Flip

Spin a wheel or flip a coin to make any decision, fast.

Stuck deciding what to eat, watch or do next? Decide It! turns any tough choice into a fun moment. Add your own options, spin the wheel and let fate pick the answer.

View on Google Play

Features

  • Decision wheel — add your own options and spin to pick a winner.
  • Coin flip — settle two-way choices with a toss.
  • Ready-made templates — what to eat, who goes first, weekend plans and more.
  • Saved lists — build and reuse lists of options for any situation.
  • Styles and sounds — pick a look that matches your mood.
  • No account, no data collection — everything stays on your device.

Free and ad-supported. An optional one-time in-app purchase removes all ads and unlocks extra spins.
